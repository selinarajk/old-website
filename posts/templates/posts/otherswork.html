Others' Work 🤩

I know that a personal website is meant to showcase your own work, but I think promoting others' work is just as important.

There are a lot of projects out there that don't get enough attention, that deserve it.

This page is meant to highlight cool projects that inspire me in my own work, and that need way more attention and love!


"Insanity" projects 😝:

An insanity project is a project that you undertake for no reason other than you want to see what you're capable of (under the pretext of seeing what the tech is capable of).

There are all kinds of insanity projects throughout history, from the building of the Pyramids to the Toronto runner who created a dancing stick man in Strava, but they are plentiful in the tech world. Below are some of my favourites.

  • Doom in Typescript types (via Simon Willison)
    • Trust me, you're going to want to watch that video (dw, it's relatively short)
    • Side note: I learned so much from the creator's explanatory comments under that video. For example, quite counterintuitively, the reason he kept going was because he was convinced from the beginning it *couldn't* be done, and in trying hard to find the reason it couldn't be done, he kept finding ways it *could* be done!
    • Another thing I learned from his comments: Very few things in life are actually hard; most of them are just hard work. The creator: "what's interesting is that there really wasn't any part of it that was 10/10 hard. there were just thousands of 8/10s"
  • Relatedly, Doom in Django
  • Pong in browser tabs
