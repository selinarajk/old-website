  3. The "Urghhh..." Challenger 👶 🧠
    4.
    An Arduino project I thought up to solve a problem I have
    • The problem: I hate processing my email inbox. Yet when I do begin the task, it's hard to stop until "finished".
    • The solution: My project rings at a specific time each evening to alert me to get ready to begin, then the LED lights change when the timer starts, and I have to finish within the given time, pushing the button when I stop. The button logs the finish time.
    • The benefits: This way I force myself to do an admin chore daily, stop at an appropriate time, and log how disciplined I'm being about stopping when I have to, helping me to get better at sticking to the end times of things I've scheduled in my calendar.
    • The delight: It also turns something I find boring and tedious to something that's now a fun challenge.
    • The possible extension: Maybe I will go further and add another pushbutton I can press every time I process an email! That would make it even more fun, and create even more logs I can analyze. (You can bet I would be pushing that pushbutton with gusto every time!)
