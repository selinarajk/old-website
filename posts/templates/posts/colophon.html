Colophon 💮

As an experiment to test what's possible with raw HTML, and to stretch my creative muscles, I decided to make this initial version of my website entirely without CSS, and with the fewest number of dependencies I could get away with.

This website and all its content was built by hand, from scratch, without AI assistance.

It has no ads and no trackers.

HTML

M1 MacBook Pro
